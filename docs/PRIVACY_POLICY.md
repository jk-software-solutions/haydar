# HADIR privacy policy template

Effective date: replace before publication.

HADIR processes company account data, employee identity and role data, attendance timestamps,
branch assignments, precise location supplied during attendance or presence verification, device
installation identifiers, tasks, requests, leave, documents, and audit records on behalf of the
customer company.

Location is collected only when the employee initiates an attendance/presence action and is used to
calculate distance from the assigned branch. Camera access is used to scan branch QR codes. Local
biometric confirmation is performed by Android; HADIR receives only a yes/no confirmation and does
not receive biometric templates or perform server-side face recognition.

Data is visible only to authorized users in the employee's company and to explicitly authorized,
audited platform support sessions. HADIR does not sell personal data. Service providers for hosting,
storage, email/push delivery, maps, and monitoring must be listed here when selected.

The customer company controls employee records and retention subject to applicable law and contract.
Security controls include encrypted transport, tenant-scoped authorization, device approval,
password hashing, protected downloads, rate limiting, backups, and audit logs. No system is risk-free;
confirmed incidents are handled under the operator's response process.

Employees should contact their company administrator for access, correction, deletion, or retention
questions. Replace this paragraph with the legal entity, jurisdiction, postal address, privacy email,
retention periods, subprocessors, international-transfer basis, and regulatory rights before using
this template as the public policy.
